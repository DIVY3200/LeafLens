# LeafLens: Plant Disease Detection

Take a photo of a leaf, or upload one, and LeafLens names the disease, shows how confident it is, and explains the symptoms, treatment and prevention.

A MobileNetV2 trained with transfer learning on PlantVillage lab photos and FieldPlant field photos (Moupojou et al., IEEE Access 2023) recognises 47 conditions across 15 crops. It is right on 86.1% of held-out field photos and 96.2% of held-out lab photos.

This Space is deployed automatically from GitHub: [DIVY3200/LeafLens](https://github.com/DIVY3200/LeafLens).
